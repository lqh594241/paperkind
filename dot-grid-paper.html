Pick the right paper.

Choose A4 for 210 × 297 mm, or US Letter for 8.5 × 11 inches. Match the PDF and your printer’s loaded paper.

02

Keep the actual size.

Select Actual size / 100% in your PDF viewer. Turn off “Fit” and “Shrink oversized pages” to preserve the spacing.

03

Check the little ruler.

Measure the 50 mm line at the bottom of your print. If it is off, check scaling and your printer before using the grid for measurements.

A FEW PAPER NOTES

About this
useful page.

Is this a ready-made journal planner?+

It is a blank dotted sheet, not a dated planner. Draw your own boxes, headings and trackers using the dots as guides.

Does the PDF contain raster dots?+

No. The dots are vector shapes, so the PDF stays crisp when viewed or printed. Print at 100% to preserve the 5 mm spacing.

Can I print a smaller notebook insert?+

The ready-made files are A4 and US Letter. Scaling to a smaller size changes the dot spacing too. These templates do not provide automatic notebook imposition.
