THE DETAIL THAT MATTERS

Match your swatch, not a universal ratio.

The ready-made sheet uses a row-height / stitch-width ratio of 0.75. For a blocked swatch with 20 stitches and 28 rows over the same distance, use 20 ÷ 28 ≈ 0.714. Enter that ratio in the generator; the default sheet does not automatically match your yarn or stitch pattern.

A FEW PAPER NOTES

About this
useful page.

Why are the cells rectangular?+

A knitted stitch is often wider than one row is tall. Rectangular cells can make a colorwork sketch resemble the finished fabric more closely, provided the ratio matches the blocked gauge.

What does the 0.75 ratio mean?+

Each cell is 5 mm wide and 3.75 mm high. The height is 0.75 times the width. This is a charting aid, not the physical size of your knitted stitch.

Does the PDF include the sample motif?+

No. Downloads are blank grids. The small colorwork example in the sharing image illustrates a possible use; it is not an included knitting pattern.
