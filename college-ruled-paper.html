A FEW PAPER NOTES

About this
useful page.

What is the college ruled spacing in millimeters?+

Exactly 7.14375 mm, which equals 9/32 inch.

Is it the same as 7 mm ruled paper?+

No. The difference is small but real: this sheet uses the inch-based 9/32-inch specification.

How do I print the correct size?+

Download the size that matches your paper. In your PDF viewer, select Actual size / 100% and turn off Fit or Shrink. Measure the 50 mm calibration ruler after printing.
