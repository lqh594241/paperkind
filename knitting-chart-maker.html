A WORKING EXAMPLE

From a swatch to a useful chart.

01

Measure like for like.

A blocked swatch has 20 stitches and 28 rows over 10 cm. A cell’s height-to-width ratio is 20 ÷ 28 = 0.7143. A 5 mm chart cell becomes about 3.57 mm tall.

02

Check the scale.

A 32-stitch, 36-row chart at that gauge represents about 16 × 12.86 cm of fabric. It is an estimate: yarn, tension, shaping and stitch pattern can change the result.

03

Read the right direction.

For conventional flat colorwork, read odd rows right to left and even rows left to right. In the round, read every row right to left. This maker charts colors; it does not generate knit/purl, lace or cable instructions.

KNOW YOUR CHART

A few useful
boundaries.

Will my project be saved automatically?+

No. Use Save project to download an editable JSON file, and Open project to continue later. Your work is held in this tab’s memory and disappears when you reload or leave.

What do the color counts mean?+

They count cells in one chart. They are not skein requirements, yarn length estimates or instructions for floats. Unpainted cells represent your chosen background yarn.

Can I make a large printed chart?+

Yes. Charts tile across A4, Letter or A5 pages at the chosen cell width, up to 30 pages. Tiles show global row and stitch numbers. Use landscape or smaller cells if you reach the page limit.

Can I keep working without signing up?+

Yes. Drawing, gauge calculations, downloads and project files run locally. No account, paid API or upload service is involved. Advertising and visit analytics are described in our privacy page.
