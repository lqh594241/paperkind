Privacy · November 11, 2026

Local processing

Paperkind processes files and inputs in your browser. The tool does not upload your content, require an account or save your inputs to local storage. Analytics is described below. Closing or reloading clears the current project. The knitting studio opens and saves editable JSON project files on your device; imported files are read locally. Chart colors, titles and stitch data are not intentionally sent to analytics or advertising services. Downloads and clipboard contents remain on your device until you remove them.

Advertising and analytics

This site uses Ahrefs Web Analytics to measure visits and site usage. Ahrefs describes this service as cookie-free. Analytics may process page URLs, referrers, browser or device information and connection data. The tool does not intentionally send your input text or file contents to Ahrefs. See Ahrefs privacy information for its data practices. This site also uses HilltopAds. The homepage and template pages load a full-width banner at the top, which you can close. Advertising popups are paused; downloading a template or custom sheet does not intentionally open an advertising tab. Advertising providers and destinations may use cookies, local storage, IP addresses, browser information and interaction data to deliver and measure ads. The generator does not intentionally send your paper settings or generated documents to them. See HilltopAds privacy information and the destination site’s privacy policy.

Hosting

Your hosting provider may process IP addresses, requested URLs and ordinary device or connection information. External links, if used, are governed by the destination site’s policies.

Your choices

Use only files you are comfortable opening on this device. You control which results you copy, print or download. You can close the banner and use browser settings or content blockers to manage advertising and popups. No file content is sent to a remote processing service by this version.
