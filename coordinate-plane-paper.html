THE DETAIL THAT MATTERS

Read the signed axis labels.

Positive x goes right and positive y goes up. The page dimensions determine the visible range. For a different numerical scale, use blank graph paper and label your own axes.

A FEW PAPER NOTES

About this
useful page.

Does each cell equal one unit?+

Yes. The ready-made axes use one coordinate unit per 5 mm cell; only even-numbered units are labeled to reduce crowding.

Why are the A4 and Letter ranges different?+

The grid keeps the same 5 mm spacing. The different physical page dimensions leave different amounts of room around the origin.

How do I print the correct size?+

Download the size that matches your paper. In your PDF viewer, select Actual size / 100% and turn off Fit or Shrink. Measure the 50 mm calibration ruler after printing.
