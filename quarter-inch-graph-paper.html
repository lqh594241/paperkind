THE DETAIL THAT MATTERS

A quarter inch is not 5 mm.

One quarter inch equals exactly 6.35 mm. At 100% print size, four cells span 25.4 mm. The heavier fifth lines are 1.25 inches apart, not one inch.

A FEW PAPER NOTES

About this
useful page.

How many squares are there per inch?+

Four in each direction when the PDF is printed at 100%. A 4 × 4 block is one square inch.

Can I use this for a room plan?+

Yes, after choosing a scale such as one square per foot. The grid does not assign a real-world scale automatically.

How do I print the correct size?+

Download the size that matches your paper. In your PDF viewer, select Actual size / 100% and turn off Fit or Shrink. Measure the 50 mm calibration ruler after printing.
